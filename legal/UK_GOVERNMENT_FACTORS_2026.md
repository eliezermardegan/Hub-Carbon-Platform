# UK Government GHG Conversion Factors 2026

## Imported factor

- Source publisher: UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ)
- Publication: 11 June 2026
- Latest page update: 31 July 2026

- Table: Table 9, base electricity generation emissions factors including imported electricity

- Reporting scope: Scope 2, location-based

## Redistribution boundary

We intentionally import only the normalized factor record required for the registry's first real-source validation. We do **not** copy the government workbook or methodology PDF into this repository.

The source workbook was not archived in this commit, so no source-file SHA-256 is asserted. The registry preserves the official source URL, publication/version metadata, table reference, licence and retrieval timestamp without inventing a binary hash.

## Methodology note

The 0.13096 kgCO2e/kWh value is the **electricity generated (supplied to the grid, plus imports)** factor. It is the location-based Scope 2 generation factor and does not include transmission/distribution losses. The methodology paper separately reports the 2026 transmission/distribution loss factor and combined electricity-consumed reference values.
